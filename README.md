#### What I build

- **[Udsyn](https://udsyn.app)**: all of Denmark on one map. Every plot, building and forest with national registers, valuations, plans, aerial photos, elevation models and point clouds joined per object, in 2D and 3D. Next.js, TypeScript, PostgreSQL/PostGIS, merge-queue CI, CodeQL, Playwright.
- **[letmcp](https://letmcp.dk)**: your business systems inside your AI in five minutes. Hosted MCP for small and mid-sized Nordic companies without an IT department: connect accounting, time tracking and case management once, give each colleague a ready-made role, and add one address in Claude, ChatGPT or Copilot. Every person only reaches what their role allows, and every action is logged without storing the answers. EU hosting, built on the open-source servers below. Next.js, TypeScript, PostgreSQL with row-level security.
- **[RIGS](https://rigs.borgels.com)**: leasing and rental of AI hardware for European businesses that want to run AI on their own data, on their own premises. DGX Spark, GPU workstations, DGX Station GB300 and rack servers on 12–36 month leases or monthly rental, with fully automated ordering (configuration, pricing, credit check, eID signing, payment), a partner portal for resellers and MSPs, and API and MCP access. Next.js, TypeScript, PostgreSQL with row-level security, deterministic pricing engine.
